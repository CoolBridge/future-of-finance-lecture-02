About the lecture

Understanding the Economy Behind Artificial Intelligence

AI is not only a technology story. It is a question of who invests, who owns the infrastructure, how productivity changes, which jobs are affected and who captures the value.

Lecture 02 brings an economist's view and a practitioner's view into the same conversation, with Nigeria as the place where the answers need to land.

Questions on the table

  • Who finances the AI revolution?
  • Who owns the infrastructure and captures the profits?
  • How will AI change productivity and employment?
  • What can Nigeria realistically capture?
  • How can AI-driven growth expand opportunity without concentrating its benefits?

Four themes

The key questions

01

The money behind AI

Capital investment, computing infrastructure, electricity, data and talent.

02

The distribution of value

Productivity, commercial returns, ownership, jobs and economic inequality.

03

The feasibility of superintelligence

What current AI can do, what remains uncertain, and the technical and economic constraints that matter.

04

Nigeria's place in the AI economy

Local applications, enterprise opportunities, skills, infrastructure and the ability to create value in African markets.

These are themes for discussion. The speakers will explore them; they will not claim a definitive answer to each.

Convener's address

Dr. John A. Olayiwola

Senior Lecturer in Accounting, Obafemi Awolowo University

Talk title

The Economics of the AI Revolution: Who Pays, Who Profits, and Where Does Nigeria Fit?

Dr. Olayiwola sets the financial and economic frame for the evening: why AI investment matters, how economic value may be created and distributed, and why Nigeria's position in the AI economy deserves attention.

  • Capital allocation and AI infrastructure
  • Productivity and the economics of adoption
  • Who captures value from AI
  • AI, jobs and financial opportunity
  • Nigeria's strategic position

Dr. Olayiwola is an accounting academic and Chartered Accountant whose research spans corporate governance, financial integrity, sustainability, financial innovation and emerging-market finance.

INVITED SPEAKER

Invited speaker

Olufemi Adeyemo

Talk title

Billions and AI: Is It Really a Game Changer?

The Feasibility and Practicality of Superintelligence (SI), and What It Means for You.

An independent presentation on whether the scale of AI investment translates into real change, and what individuals and organisations in Nigeria can do with AI today.

  • AI investment and infrastructure
  • The case for and against AI as a game changer
  • What superintelligence would require: computing, electricity, data and safety
  • Practicality and affordability in the Nigerian context
  • Applications in agriculture, healthcare, education and finance
  • Building frontier models versus applying existing models to local problems

Where the talk draws on estimates or personal judgement, the speaker will say so.
